VR editor, seen from inside the headset

First person at 1.6 m eye height. Drag to look around, click a component to select it, click the dock's tabs in 3D. For Danny (UI) and Evan (backend).

What's on screen

  1. 1
    Editor Dock. Left side, 35° off forward, 55 cm out, tilted to face you. 36 × 46 cm. Library adds things, Inspect tweaks the selection, Layers shows frames and their children, World holds the room, scenes, view toggles and undo. Its tabs are clickable in 3D.
  2. 2
    Assistant. Mirrors the dock on the right. A terminal-style log shows what you said (›) and each op it ran (● … ✓), with hold-to-talk and four quick chips.
  3. 3
    Pix. A 14 cm voxel guide perched on the Assistant. Blinks, bobs, always faces you. Its face is where voice state (listening, thinking) would show.
  4. 4
    Selection. Mint outline, X/Y/Z move arrows, mint yaw ring, warm tilt ring, corner cubes for uniform scale. The yaw ring shows 15° ticks while Snap is on.
  5. 5
    Track wall. A frame: three lanes on a 1.6 m arc. The lanes keep facing you and the frame moves as one piece.
  6. 6
    X-ray. The room fades, objects turn to glass with wire edges, frame bounds appear, and glowing cables join bound values: tempo knob → tempo display, pads → drums lane, keys → keys lane.
  7. 7
    Grid & Snap. A floor grid for placement. Snap moves in 5 cm steps and turns in 15° steps.

Open questions for Danny

  1. Dock follow. Should the dock stay world-locked where you leave it (shown), lazily re-centre after you turn about 50°, or attach to your wrist? Should the Assistant follow the same rule?
  2. Sizes. Rows here are 32 mm tall, about 3.3° of view at 55 cm. Should we spec targets in mm or degrees rather than "≥ 44 px"? Is a 36 × 46 cm panel too big at arm's length?
  3. Poke vs pinch. The dock is within arm's reach, so poke works there. The track wall at 1.6 m needs a pinch ray. Should panels support both, and should targets grow when you aim with a ray?
  4. Mascot spot. Should Pix sit on the Assistant (shown), hover near the selection, or stay at the edge of view? Should it ever cover content?
  5. Occlusion. At 35° the dock hides the left half of the keys. Should the panel fade when you look past it, or move out to 45°?

For Evan

  • Each ● line is one op on the command bus (set_prop, scale, bind). Dock buttons should send the same ops so that Undo is a single stack.
  • The X-ray cables are the bind table drawn in space, so the client will need a "list bindings" call.
